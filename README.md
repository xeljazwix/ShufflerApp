## What is Shuffler?

Shuffler is a free, community-built home for everything you want to watch: movies, TV series and anime, all in one clean, fast app. Browse what's trending, pick up exactly where you left off, start watching in seconds, and now watch together with your friends, on your phone, your tablet, your TV or your PC.

No clutter. No confusing menus. Just point, pick and press play.

## 🆕 New in 2.0

### ✂️ A whole new look
A new logo (a play button, cut in two), a new icon set and new layouts on every screen. Home deals you a fanned deck of picks, a Top 10 chart and wide spotlight cards. Title pages are built around the artwork, with a big Play button and tabs for Episodes, About, More like this and Community. Discover goes by mood, and My List is a poster wall that shows where you are in each title. Every tap answers with a small sound and a buzz, and Theme Studio can give anime a colour of its own.

### 🍿 Watch parties
Start a party, send the invite link or code, and everyone watches the same moment. The host, and any co-hosts they pick, play, pause and skip for the room; everyone else follows and is nudged back into step if they drift. Chat with GIFs, vote on what to watch next, call a ready check before you press play, or schedule a party up to 30 days ahead. Watch the host's stream and subtitles, or pick your own quality, subtitles and audio. Parties replace Live TV, on the phone, Android TV and Windows.

### 👥 Profiles, friends, the feed and the lounge
A profile with a cover photo, your favourite four and your watch stats. Add friends, earn 30 achievements in bronze, silver and gold, and choose who sees each part of your profile. Post what you think with a title, a GIF and a spoiler blur; react, comment, share and quote. Your ratings and reviews can post themselves. The lounge is one public chat room for everyone on Shuffler.

### 🔁 AniList, both ways
Connect your AniList account (on a TV, with a QR code) and your list and progress follow you: finished episodes count there, and what you watch on AniList shows up in Continue Watching. Plus trailers, ranking badges, tags, and pages for characters, studios and tags.

### 💬 Subtitles that sort themselves out
No subtitles in your language? The best match for that exact release downloads by itself, or your second language if there's none. Out of step with the video? They're lined up for you, with Undo. Shuffler remembers the subtitle you used for every episode, and never lays one over subtitles already burned into the picture.

### 📱 Your phone is the TV remote
On the same Wi-Fi, your phone finds your Shuffler TV. Pick something on the phone and it plays on the TV. Use the remote pad or a touchpad, type searches, and change audio, subtitles, quality, speed and episodes, even from a floating button over other apps.

### ▶️ A sharper player
A preview frame while you scrub, the intro, recap and credits marked on the seek bar, a volume boost and pinch to zoom. The next episode stays on the source you were watching, and the quality list shows the other sources too.

### 📐 Made for every screen
Tablets get their own layout, with two panes when held sideways. Text and layout keep their size whatever your font settings say. On low-end TVs, weak TV mode keeps things smooth, turned on by itself when the TV needs it.

## ✨ What's inside

### 🎬 A library that never runs out
Trending, popular, top-rated and newly released movies, series and anime, all beautifully organized and instantly searchable. Every title comes with real IMDb ratings, a poster-first browsing experience, and an "In Theaters" badge so you always know what's fresh.

### 🔀 Can't decide? Shuffle.
Open **Shuffler** from the centre button, answer a few quick questions (your mood, how much time you have, who you're watching with) and **Shuffler AI** picks a movie, a series, or even a single episode for you, revealed with a poster shuffle. Pick one or more titles you love to get something similar, and get anime-specific questions when you're in the anime section. Bring your own key for OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, or open-source models through OpenRouter or Groq.

### ⭐ Rate, review and discuss
Rate any movie, series or anime from 1 to 10, write reviews with spoiler tags, and talk about whole titles or single episodes in their own discussions. Discussions are shared with our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/pje9Tdu4m3): posts from the app appear there, and replies from Discord appear in the app. Link your Discord account in Settings to get your supporter role automatically.

### 🌸 Anime, with a home of its own
Anime has its own section, completely separate from movies and series: its own Home, Discover and Search, its own watch history, My List and episode calendar, and its own colours.
- **Sub or Dub** for every episode and anime movie, right in the source picker.
- **Studios, characters and tags**, each with its own page, and Discover filters by tag and country.
- **Skip Intro, Outro and Recap**, powered by AniSkip.
- **Its own languages**: set anime audio and subtitle languages separately from everything else.
- **Download episodes** one at a time, or select several and grab them all in one go.

### 🎯 Built around your taste
Tell Shuffler what you like once, and your own **For You** row keeps getting smarter: genuinely tailored picks, not generic "trending" filler. "Add to your tastes" is on every title page. Prefer full control? Choose which genre rows appear on your Home screen, and in what order.

### 📡 Watch your way
Shuffler doesn't lock you into one source. Choose the streaming method that fits you:
- **Direct streaming**: a fast, high-quality source with clear tags for resolution, HDR/HEVC, dual audio and more.
- **Peer-to-peer streaming**: for the widest possible selection.
- **CloudStream plugins**: power users can browse and install community add-ons straight from an in-app repo store.

### ⏭️ Playback that respects your time
- **Up Next**: a small card appears as an episode wraps up, and the next one plays from the same source.
- **Skip Intro, Recap and Outro**: real, community-sourced skip points, with an optional auto-skip mode.
- **Resume, exactly**: pick up a movie or episode right where you paused it, every time.
- **Subtitles that look right**: download subtitles for the exact episode you're watching, and fine-tune their size, colour and position live inside the player.

### ✅ Keep track of everything
- **Watched, or not yet**: episodes are marked watched in their last 3 minutes, or mark them yourself, in sync across your devices.
- **Always on the right episode**: title pages open on the episode you're up to, and Continue Watching moves on to the next episode by itself.
- **Watch status**: every title in My List has a status (Plan to Watch, Watching, Completed, and Paused and Dropped for anime), and your lists show up as rows on Home.
- **Episode calendar**: see when new episodes of the shows in your list come out, and get a notification the moment one lands.

### ⬇️ Take it offline
Download movies, full seasons, single episodes, or a handful of anime episodes at once. The Downloads page shows each download's live progress and when it will finish, groups your shows by season, and lets you delete a whole show, a season or a single episode, always with a confirmation first. Downloads keep going in the background and pause on mobile data if you'd rather wait for Wi-Fi.

### 🔔 Stay in the loop
Notifications for friend requests, comments, replies, reactions, achievements and parties, plus news from the Shuffler team. When a new version is out, Shuffler finds it and installs it for you, and a **What's New** popup shows exactly what changed. Settings → Guide explains every player control, gesture and setting.

### 🌍 Made to feel like home
Full Arabic language support with a proper right-to-left layout, alongside English, with more languages on the way.

## 🖥️ On Windows

**Shuffler for Windows 2.0** is a real desktop app built for Windows from the ground up, not a phone app in a window. Same account, same library, same sources: sign in and your My List, watch history and settings are already there.

- **Everything from 2.0**: the new look, watch parties, profiles and friends, the feed, the lounge, AniList, subtitles that download and sync themselves, and the sharper player with scrub previews.
- **Everything from the phone app**: movies, series and the full anime section with Sub and Dub, For You, your genre rows, My List with watch status, the episode calendar, Continue Watching, Shuffler AI, ratings, reviews and discussions, and downloads.
- **Made for a mouse and keyboard**: a sidebar that's always one click away, poster grids that fill however wide your window is, and **Ctrl+K** to search anything from anywhere.
- **A proper desktop player**: Space to pause, arrow keys to seek and change the volume, F or a double-click for fullscreen, M to mute, L for an A-B loop, S for a screenshot. Subtitles (with automatic translation when none exist in your language), audio tracks, playback speed and the episode list open right beside the video.
- **Feels at home on Windows**: media keys and the Windows media overlay control playback, download progress shows on the taskbar icon, Shuffler lives in the system tray, and parties, friends and new episodes arrive as Windows notifications.
- **Updates itself**: when a new version is out, the Update screen downloads and runs the new installer.
- **Opens what you throw at it**: `magnet:` links, and `.mkv`, `.mp4`, `.avi` and `.srt` files through "Open with".

### Install it on your PC

Download **ShufflerSetup-2.0.0.exe** from the [**Releases**](https://github.com/xeljazwix/ShufflerApp/releases) page and run it. It installs just for you, with no administrator rights needed. Windows 10 (version 1809) or newer, 64-bit.

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning the first time, because the installer isn't signed yet: choose **More info → Run anyway**. Already using an older Shuffler for Windows? The new installer upgrades it in place, and keeps your sign-in, settings and downloads.

## 📺 Android TV

A full TV experience, built for the couch and a remote control, not a stretched phone app.

- **The 2.0 look, made for the remote**: a Top 10 chart and genre tiles on Home, the new title pages, and the anime section with its characters.
- **Watch parties**: join a party from the TV, with the chat on your phone through a QR code. From the phone, send a party straight to the TV.
- **Your phone is the remote**: pick on the phone and it plays on the TV, with a touchpad, phone typing, and audio, subtitle and quality controls.
- **Plays everything**: HEVC (x265), 10-bit, HDR and AV1 through your TV's own hardware decoder, at your screen's full resolution, with a quality selector right in the player.
- **Subtitles that sort themselves out**, plus a subtitle delay.
- **Sign in in seconds**: scan the QR code on your TV with your phone, or enter the code on the website. No typing passwords with a remote.
- **Weak TV mode**: on low-end TVs, lighter pictures and a smaller video buffer keep things smooth, turned on by itself when the TV needs it.
- **Idle mode**: leave the TV alone for a while and Shuffler turns into a full-screen slideshow of movies, series and anime.
- **Your home screen, your style**: pick a black, white, green or anime-coloured Shuffler banner for your TV's home screen, or the special anime banner.
